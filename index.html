Testing & Policy

How we test (editable)

  • Visual inspection: ports, screws, heatsink, fans, oxidation.
  • Power-on check: driver detection, VRAM capacity readout, PCIe link.
  • Stress test: 10–20 minutes; record peak temperature & stability symptoms.
  • Stability: benchmark + a real game/workload to catch artifacts/crashes.
  • Final check before handoff/shipping (cleaning optional).

Warranty & deal terms (editable)

  • 7-day functional guarantee: if it arrives DOA / non-functional under normal use (non-human damage), we will work it out.
  • Condition grading: A=like new; B=light wear; C=visible wear, fully functional.
  • Pickup or shipping: pickup recommended; for shipping, record an unboxing video.
  • Accessories: GPU only unless otherwise stated (box/receipt will be noted).
Used hardware naturally carries wear risk. Best practice: disclose test method/time/results and any known issues per unit.

FAQ

Are these ex-mining cards?

You can disclose the source per unit (personal upgrade / studio decommission / etc.). If you do not sell ex-mining cards, state that clearly. Always be truthful and keep test notes.

Will it fit my PSU/case?

Add per-item details like suggested PSU wattage, power connectors, and card length. Buyers can share CPU/PSU/case constraints for a quick check.

Can I test in person?

Yes. Book a time and agree on what to test (game/benchmark/render) to avoid setup delays.

Do you allow deposits/reservations?

Yes. Mark items as “Reserved” and specify the hold period and rules.
